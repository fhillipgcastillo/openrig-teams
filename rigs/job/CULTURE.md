# Independent job culture

One job has one rig and one assigned workspace, whether existing checkout or managed worktree. The job lead is the human-facing owner of that assignment. Optional fleet management routes requests across jobs and does not replace job leads.

Use native OpenRig identity, queues, messaging, and handover. Project instructions govern project work. Keep evidence and unresolved questions durable. A failed daemon read means unknown state; it does not prove missing rigs or authorize replacement.

Do not commit, push, merge, create release metadata, or start/stop/reset user services. Workspace creation/cleanup is through the job wrappers and explicit authorization only. Never approve another seat's permission prompts. Respect project isolation constraints and dependency gates.

Answer the requested scope with evidence, identify limitations, and preserve user work. Shared learning/memory and environment scheduling are outside this implementation.
